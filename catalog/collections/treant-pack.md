under the `treant` family.

| `IMG-CONCEPT-000019` | concept | Ancient treant boss, full body in a misty forest |

| `IMG-CONCEPT-000021` | concept | Close-up treant face for portraits and icons |

The family collapses into one gallery card with four thumbnails. Search `treant`
to find it.
