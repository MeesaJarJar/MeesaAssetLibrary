# Dog Pack

A cross-modal starter set: everything currently tagged `subject:creature/dog`.
Use it to test `dog` search, family collapse and pack export.

| ID | Kind | Role | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `IMG-CONCEPT-000013` | image | concept | Brown dog sitting, full body, family `dog` |
| `IMG-CONCEPT-000014` | image | concept | Golden retriever running, family `dog` |
| `IMG-CONCEPT-000015` | image | concept | White terrier portrait, family `dog` |
| `SND-SFX-000016` | audio | sfx | Two natural dog barks, 4 s |
| `VID-CAPTURE-000017` | video | capture | Camera loop over the running retriever, 6 s |
| `MDL-PROP-000018` | model | prop | 65,536-gaussian splat of the sitting dog |

The three images share the `dog` family and collapse into one gallery card with
three variants. The audio, video and model stay separate so each modality is
visible in the results.
