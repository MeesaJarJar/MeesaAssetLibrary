# Brown Dog Splat

65,536-gaussian 3D reconstruction of the sitting brown dog, exported as PLY. Use in the gallery splat viewer or as a stylized 3D companion reference; it is a Gaussian splat, not a rigged mesh.
- Use for: reference
- Technical: application/octet-stream
- Notes: ComfyUI TripoSplat from IMG-CONCEPT-000013 dog-sitting.png, 65,536 gaussians. The generated preview is rotated 180 degrees on X; the PLY needs Flip Y on in the viewer.
