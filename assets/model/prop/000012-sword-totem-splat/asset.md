# Sword Totem Splat

32,768-splat Gaussian reconstruction of a tall sword-shaped totem with a pale face at the guard, roughly 5.8 to 1 tall. Use to exercise the 3D splat viewer and as a stylized weapon or shrine prop reference; the PLY is the original and the poster is a viewport render.
- Use for: reference
- Technical: application/octet-stream
- Notes: Source jarjar_test.ply from the local ply-splat-viewer set. 32,768 gaussians, binary little-endian PLY. Preview is a render from the gallery splat viewer.
