# Health Potion Icon

32x32 flat-vector flask: red liquid, tan cork, one white highlight and a 1px dark outline; legible down to 16px.
Use in inventory grids, quick slots, pickup prompts and tooltips for a health consumable.
Transparent background with no slot frame, badge or count text - the UI layer owns those.
Recolor by changing the two reds (#c8323c, #8f1f2a) and keep the outline dark for contrast on light bars.
Scales cleanly at integer sizes; fractional scaling blurs the outline.
