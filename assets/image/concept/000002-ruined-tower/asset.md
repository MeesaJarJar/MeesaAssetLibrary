# Ruined Tower on a Hill

Moody painted key art at 512x384: a broken stone tower on a dark hill under an indigo-to-amber dusk sky, with two faint lit slits.
Use as a concept or mood plate for title screens, loading art and ruined-tower blockouts; the left third is open for text.
Not a game-ready background: the layers are flat, there is no parallax separation, and the tower is one merged silhouette rather than parts.
The cold-to-warm sky ramp is the main reusable idea; pair it with DOC-PALETTE-000005 and IMG-TILE-000001.
Reads at thumbnail size, so it also works as a collection or store card.
