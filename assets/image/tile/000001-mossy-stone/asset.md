# Mossy Stone Tile

Seamless 64x64 cobblestone in cold grey-green, moss packed into the joints; low internal contrast keeps repeated tiles from sparkling at 1x.
Use for overgrown ruins, crypt floors, forest paths and courtyard ground in top-down or side-on 2D at integer scale.
Avoid clean or sci-fi architecture: the moss and irregular joints read as age, not as a neutral stone material.
Stones wrap across the left and right edges, so repeat the tile rather than rescaling it; fractional scale blurs the 1px joints.
Pairs with DOC-PALETTE-000005 for matching ground props and UI accents.
