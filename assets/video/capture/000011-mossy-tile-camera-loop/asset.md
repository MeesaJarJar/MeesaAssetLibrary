# Mossy Tile Camera Loop

Six-second 1280x720 camera drift across the mossy cobblestone texture, pushing in slowly with a gentle sway. Use to demonstrate video playback or as a placeholder ambience plate; the still it was rendered from is the tileable texture bundle.
- Use for: reference
- Technical: video/mp4
- Notes: Rendered with ffmpeg from IMG-TEXTURE-000009 (Albedo_00001_.png), 24 fps, H.264, CRF 20, no audio track.
