# Mossloop Ambience

Six-second stereo ambient bed of stacked low sine tones (G3, D4, G4) with slow tremolo, echo and fade in/out. Use as a quiet forest or menu loop; the fade makes it loop cleanly at six seconds.
- Use for: ambience
- Technical: audio/ogg
- Notes: Synthesized with ffmpeg for the viewer demo; deterministic recipe, no samples or external audio.
