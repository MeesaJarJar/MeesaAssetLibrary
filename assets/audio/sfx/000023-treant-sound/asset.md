# Treant Sound

A four-second one-shot sound effect for a treant, dry and unprocessed. Use as a first-pass cue; trim and layer it for the final mix.
- Use for: sfx
- Technical: duration_s=4, sample_rate=44100, channels=2, loopable=false
- Notes: Prototype pack for the queue request "treant". Stable Audio 3, 4 s.
